# Reference rationale

The reference searches simple directed routes, combines each with an allowed wave, and jointly chooses acceptance or rejection. Feasibility includes whole-order lane capacity, cumulative transit time, cumulative exposure, and a separate shared cooling-energy budget per wave. Each opened lane is charged once across the shift; pallet costs are charged on every traversed lane. The objective is the priority-count tuple, total cost, then the specified route/wave descriptor.

Independent behavior axes and their checks:

- `PRI-01`: lexicographic service before cost (`test_hand_computed_dispatch_respects_priority_and_open_fee_gate`, `test_exhaustive_small_ledgers_match_independent_joint_enumeration`, `test_unreachable_order_is_rejected_without_changing_other_service`). A hand case independently checks the priority conflict.
- `CAP-02`: indivisible orders over shared, per-wave lane capacity (`test_whole_order_cannot_be_split_across_individually_insufficient_paths`, `test_partial_capacity_assignment_prefers_feasible_order_subset`). The independent enumerator checks all route/reject combinations and recomputes aggregate loads.
- `THERM-09`: exposure is a separate route bound from transit SLA (`test_exposure_boundary_selects_a_feasible_route`). The explicit hand fixture rejects a direct route with six exposure minutes and selects a two-lane route at the five-minute boundary while meeting the four-minute SLA.
- `COOL-10`: cooling units are pallet-weighted and pooled within, but not across, waves (`test_wave_energy_is_bounded_per_wave_and_shared_by_orders`). Two early orders compete for one wave budget while a late order uses its independent budget; expected service counts, ledgers and cost are calculated by hand.
- `FIX-03`: one-time lane activation plus per-pallet route costs (`test_shared_lane_fee_is_paid_once_for_multiple_orders`, `test_opening_fee_can_overrule_individually_cheapest_lanes`). Expected charges are independently recomputed.
- `TIE-04`: global deterministic path and wave tie ordering (`test_tie_break_uses_lane_ids_not_input_lane_order`, `test_tie_break_is_stable_under_order_input_permutation`).
- `SLA-07` and `WAVE-08`: transit boundary, wave eligibility, separate lane capacity, and shift-wide activation (`test_sla_boundary_selects_a_feasible_route_and_rejects_late_only_service`, `test_dispatch_waves_have_separate_capacity_and_one_shift_opening_fee`).
- `VALID-05`: strict schema, late rejection, atomic replace failure and stable output (`test_rejects_duplicate_json_key_without_creating_output`, `test_rejects_boolean_capacity_and_preserves_sentinel`, `test_rejects_fractional_integer_field`, `test_rejects_duplicate_identifier_and_late_invalid_order_atomically`, `test_atomic_replace_failure_preserves_old_report`, `test_output_shape_and_input_order_are_preserved`, `test_identical_input_produces_identical_plan`). The replace-failure fixture is valid and injects failure at `os.replace`.
- `SCALE-06`: the dense layered-network fixture verifies a cut upper bound against an attained assignment (`test_dense_route_batch_uses_shared_capacity_and_global_tie_break`). Its expected optimum is derived independently of the candidate solver.

The suite has 21 discoverable tests: six natural completion gates and 15 weighted cases. The independent small-instance oracle does not call the candidate solver. `assert_plan_consistent` recomputes path continuity, node uniqueness, SLA and exposure, wave eligibility, indivisibility, lane and energy loads, service counts and all costs. Candidate execution for the current unpublished edit is pending; these counts describe the authored suite, not a passing run.
